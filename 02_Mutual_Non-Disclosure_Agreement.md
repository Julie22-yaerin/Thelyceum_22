# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [DATE] (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between:

[YOUR COMPANY / ENTITY NAME] ("**Disclosing/Receiving Party A**"), and

[COUNTERPARTY COMPANY NAME] ("**Disclosing/Receiving Party B**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving the licensing, evaluation, and/or purchase of software and source code developed by [YOUR COMPANY NAME] (the "**Purpose**"), which may require each Party to disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to the other.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**2.1** "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Disclosing Party**") to the other (the "**Receiving Party**"), whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by inspection, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure, including but not limited to:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) **source code, object code, algorithms, architecture, technical specifications, APIs, and system designs**;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) business plans, pricing, financial information, and customer/prospect data;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) product roadmaps, know-how, trade secrets, and unpublished patent applications; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) the existence, terms, and status of discussions between the Parties.

**2.2** For the avoidance of doubt, any **source code** provided by the Disclosing Party for demonstration, evaluation, or due-diligence purposes is Confidential Information, regardless of whether it is separately marked as such.

---

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar nature, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party, except to its employees, officers, and professional advisors who have a strict need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code or underlying structure of any software disclosed under this Agreement, except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by applicable law; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Confidential Information of which it becomes aware.

---

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written record:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) was already lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction on disclosure.

If the Receiving Party is compelled by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt prior written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order, and shall disclose only the portion of Confidential Information legally required.

---

## 5. No License; No Ownership Transfer

**5.1** Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting the Receiving Party any license, right, title, or interest in or to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, including any source code, other than the limited right to review it for the Purpose.

**5.2** All Confidential Information remains the sole property of the Disclosing Party. No Party is obligated under this Agreement to enter into any further agreement, license, purchase, or transaction with the other Party.

---

## 6. Term

**6.1** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for [TWO (2)] years, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

**6.2** The confidentiality obligations in Sections 3 and 5 shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of [FIVE (5)] years, provided that obligations with respect to source code and trade secrets shall survive for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, or upon termination of discussions between the Parties, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all copies of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession, including source code, and certify such destruction in writing if requested, except for archival copies retained solely as required by law or by the Receiving Party's standard backup/retention policies (which shall remain subject to this Agreement).

---

## 8. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information — particularly source code — may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the need to post a bond.

---

This Agreement shall be governed by generally recognized principles of international commercial law. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be finally settled by binding arbitration under the **UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules**, with seat, arbitrator(s), and language to be mutually agreed by the Parties in writing.
