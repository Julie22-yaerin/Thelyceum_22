# Pricing tiers — the only prices an agent may quote

| Tier       | Price (USD) | Billing   |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Standard   | 299         | per month |
| Enterprise | 2500        | per month |

No other number is a price. Discounts do not exist unless a human records one
against the account. An agent that invents a price is a fact-check failure and
must be rejected, not corrected in place.

# Gross margin target

60% after inference cost. Inference cost per request is measured, not
estimated — see the unit-economics log attached to every proxied call.
