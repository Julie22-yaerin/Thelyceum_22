# Positioning

The problem: companies are hiring AI agents with no operations layer. No
budget ceiling, no audit trail, no way to stop a loop at 3am.

The Lyceum is that layer. It sits between the agents and the model providers.

# Rules for outreach

- Quote only prices from the finance department's published tiers. If the
  figure is not in your retrieved context, you do not have it — ask a human.
- Never promise a roadmap item.
- Never claim a named customer.
