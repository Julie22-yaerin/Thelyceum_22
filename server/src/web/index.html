The circuit breaker company

We stop AI before it costs you.

Two things, done narrowly and well: brake stops a dangerous action in under a second. redteam attacks a one-sided conclusion before it's handed to you as settled. Both are always-on — the model calls them itself, no slash command, no asking.

This category is about to get crowded with "optimize your AI spend" tools that watch dashboards after the damage is done. We don't watch. We stop things, measured, with the SLA reported even when we miss it.

brake

paid

The emergency brake for AI agents. Stops the model before it exfiltrates data, attacks infrastructure, reads a credential, or moves money — 1000ms SLA, measured and reported. A brake that quietly took 3 seconds while the UI said 1000ms would be worse than no brake, so a miss is shown, never hidden. 

npm install -g brake
brake install all

redteam

free forever

An adversarial rebuttal layer for AI reasoning. Scans a claim or plan for overconfidence, unsupported claims, confirmation bias, false dichotomies, missing trade-offs — then steelmans the other side, so a conclusion gets tested instead of asserted.

Pricing

redteam is free, no account, forever — it's MIT and nothing about it needs a server. brake's core detection is the same: free, MIT, works today with no license. What you pay for is tracked devices, the guided setup below, and priority support — not the danger detection itself. We'd rather say that plainly than let the price imply more than it buys.

All prices in USD. Cancel anytime. Annual billed up-front as the per-month equivalent × 12. The 1000ms SLA is measured — a slow brake is reported as over-SLA, never hidden.
